Scrambled Eggs — Calories & Nutrition

🛒 Ingredients (1 serving)

  • Eggs2 large
  • Butter1 tsp (5g)
  • Whole milk1 tbsp (15ml)
  • Salt¼ tsp
  • Black pepperpinch

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. 1
    Crack eggs into a bowl. Add milk, salt and pepper. Whisk thoroughly until yolks and whites are fully combined and slightly foamy.
    💡 Adding milk makes eggs slightly fluffier and more creamy.
  2. 2
    Melt butter in a non-stick pan over low-medium heat. Don't let it brown.
  3. 3
    Pour in egg mixture. Let it sit for 20 seconds until edges just start to set.
    💡 Low heat is the secret to creamy, soft scrambled eggs. High heat makes them rubbery.
  4. 4
    Using a spatula, gently push eggs from the edges toward the centre in slow folds. Remove from heat while still slightly wet — they will continue cooking from residual heat.
  5. 5
    Serve immediately. Garnish with chives or fresh herbs if desired.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories in 2 scrambled eggs?
2 large scrambled eggs cooked with 1 tsp butter contain approximately 220 kcal, 14g protein, 1g carbs and 17g fat. Without butter: ~155 kcal using a non-stick pan.
How many calories in 3 scrambled eggs?
3 large scrambled eggs with butter: approximately 300 kcal, 21g protein, 1g carbs and 23g fat.
Are scrambled eggs good for weight loss?
Yes — eggs are high in protein which increases satiety. Studies suggest eating eggs for breakfast may reduce calorie intake throughout the day. Scrambled eggs without butter are only ~155 kcal for 2 eggs.
Are scrambled eggs high in protein?
2 large eggs provide approximately 14g of protein — making scrambled eggs an excellent high-quality protein source. The protein in eggs is highly bioavailable and contains all essential amino acids.
How many calories in scrambled eggs with cheese?
Add 30g cheddar cheese: +120 kcal, +7g protein. Total for 2 eggs + cheese: approximately 340 kcal, 21g protein.
How many calories in scrambled eggs with butter and milk?
2 eggs + 1 tsp butter + 1 tbsp whole milk = approximately 220 kcal. Using skimmed milk saves about 5 kcal; skipping milk makes no significant calorie difference.
Can I eat scrambled eggs on keto?
Yes — eggs are one of the best keto foods. 2 scrambled eggs with butter contain only 1g carbs, making them ideal for any ketogenic diet.
How do you make scrambled eggs without butter?
Use a good non-stick pan on low heat with no fat, or use cooking spray (adds ~5 kcal). The result is slightly less rich but saves approximately 35 kcal per serving.
