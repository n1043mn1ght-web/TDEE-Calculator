Egg White Benefits

Purest Protein Food Available

Egg white provides 10.9g of protein at only 52 kcal per 100g — a protein-to-calorie ratio of 0.84, with 84% of total calories from protein. One large egg white delivers 3.6g of complete protein at only 17 calories. For athletes or dieters seeking maximum protein with minimum calories, egg white is effectively unbeatable among whole foods.

Albumin — Unique Protein Properties

Egg white protein is primarily ovalbumin, ovotransferrin, and ovomucin — albumin proteins with unique functional properties. Ovalbumin is the reference protein for measuring other proteins' nutritional value. These proteins form a stable foam when beaten, creating unique culinary applications. Ovotransferrin has antimicrobial properties by binding to iron that bacteria need to grow.

Weight Loss Applications

The combination of high protein (84% of calories) and very low total calories makes egg whites exceptional for calorie-restricted diets. Adding two egg whites to a meal adds only 34 calories while providing 7.2g of protein — significantly increasing the protein-to-calorie ratio of the overall meal and improving satiety without adding meaningful calories.

Biotin Consideration

Raw egg white contains avidin — a protein that binds biotin (vitamin B7) with extraordinarily high affinity, blocking its absorption. Eating raw egg whites daily can cause biotin deficiency over time. Cooking completely denatures avidin, eliminating this concern. All culinary applications involving cooked egg white are safe; raw egg white consumption in very large quantities warrants caution.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in an egg white?
One large egg white (33g) has 17 calories. Per 100g: 52 calories. Two egg whites: 34 calories with 7.2g protein.
Is egg white better than whole egg?
Depends on goal. Egg white has fewer calories and fat. Whole egg has more vitamins, choline, and healthy fats. For pure protein with minimum calories: egg white. For overall nutrition: whole egg.
How much protein in egg white?
10.9g per 100g. One large egg white: 3.6g. Two egg whites: 7.2g — comparable to many protein supplements.
Is raw egg white safe to eat?
Raw egg white contains avidin which blocks biotin absorption, and carries salmonella risk. Cooking denatures avidin and kills bacteria. Cooked egg white is safer and has higher protein bioavailability (91% cooked vs 51% raw).
Is egg white keto?
Yes — 0.73g carbs per 100g. Very low calorie and virtually carb-free.
Can you eat only egg whites for weight loss?
Egg whites are very effective for weight loss — low calorie, high protein. However, whole eggs provide essential nutrients (choline, B12, vitamin D) that egg whites lack. A combination of whole eggs and egg whites is optimal.
