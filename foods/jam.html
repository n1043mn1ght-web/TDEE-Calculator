Jam Nutrition Facts

Concentrated Sugar — The Processing Effect

Making jam involves heating fruit to 104°C with large amounts of sugar. This process destroys most heat-sensitive vitamins (vitamin C reduced by 50-80%) and degrades polyphenol antioxidants. The resulting product retains some pectin, potassium, and trace minerals, but is primarily sugar with fruit flavour.

Pectin — Modest Fiber Benefit

Jam's setting agent is pectin — a soluble fiber. While pectin has cholesterol-lowering and gut health benefits, the amount in a typical jam serving (1 tablespoon) is approximately 0.2g — too low to provide meaningful benefits. Reduced-sugar jams (which need more pectin to set) actually have slightly more pectin.

Reduced-Sugar Alternatives

Reduced-sugar jam (~160 kcal, 36g sugar) achieves lower calorie content by using more pectin and sugar substitutes. 100% fruit spreads have no added sugar but still contain concentrated fruit sugars (48g). Chia seed jam is the most nutritious alternative: ~80 kcal, 2-3g fiber, no added sugar, retaining original fruit nutrients.

Practical Calorie Management

A thin scrape (10g, 28 kcal) saves 28 kcal per day vs a full tablespoon. Choosing reduced-sugar jam saves 19 kcal per tablespoon — equivalent to ~2kg fat loss potential over a year if maintained daily. Small, consistent reductions in condiment use compound significantly over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in jam?
Strawberry jam has 278 calories per 100g. 1 tablespoon (20g): 56 kcal. A thin scrape (10g): 28 kcal.
Is jam healthy?
Standard jam is essentially fruit-flavoured sugar with limited nutritional value. As a condiment in small amounts (1 thin scrape, ~28 kcal), it's harmless. As a regular daily addition, the sugar content adds up.
What is the healthiest jam?
Reduced-sugar jam: ~160 kcal, 36g sugar. 100% fruit spread: ~210 kcal, 48g natural sugars (lower GI). Chia jam (fresh fruit + chia seeds): ~80-100 kcal/100g, 3g fiber — best option.
Jam vs marmalade?
Very similar: marmalade ~260 kcal, 66g sugar. The peel adds some pectin and vitamin C. Nutritionally nearly identical.
Is jam keto?
No — 68.9g carbs per 100g.
How to make healthier jam?
Chia seed jam: blend fresh/frozen fruit, heat briefly, add 2 tbsp chia seeds — sets naturally with much less sugar. Result: ~80-100 kcal/100g with 3g fiber per tablespoon.
