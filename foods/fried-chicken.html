Fried Chicken Nutrition Facts

High Protein — Best of Fast Foods

At 26g protein per 100g, fried chicken has one of the highest protein contents of any fast food. This complete protein — providing all 9 essential amino acids — makes fried chicken a meaningful protein source despite its other nutritional limitations. The protein also produces strong satiety, meaning people tend to eat less in subsequent meals after a protein-rich fried chicken meal compared to lower-protein fast food.

The Frying Effect

The breading on fried chicken acts like a sponge during deep frying, absorbing substantial amounts of cooking oil. This oil absorption accounts for the ~80-100 kcal/100g difference between fried and roasted chicken. Different frying methods produce different oil absorption: pressure frying (used by KFC) absorbs less oil than open pan frying, producing a crispier crust. Air frying mimics the Maillard reaction (browning) without submersion in oil, cutting fat by 70-75%.

Sodium from Seasoning

Most fried chicken recipes involve brining or marinating in salt solutions, seasoned coatings, and sometimes monosodium glutamate — all contributing to the 502mg sodium per 100g. Fast food fried chicken is typically higher than homemade due to these processing steps. Reducing sodium means limiting marinade salt, using herb-based seasonings, and controlling coating salt.

Skin and Breading — Remove for Calories

The skin and breading of fried chicken contains the majority of the fat and most of the calorie increase over plain chicken. Removing both before eating saves 30-40% of calories. A 160g fried chicken breast without skin or breading: ~240 kcal vs ~416 kcal with skin and breading — a 170 kcal saving from a single modification.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in fried chicken?
Fried chicken has 260 calories per 100g. A drumstick (70g): 182 kcal. A large breast piece (~160g): ~416 kcal. KFC Original Recipe breast: 390 kcal.
Is fried chicken healthy?
Fried chicken is a significant source of protein (26g/100g) but the frying process adds substantial fat and calories compared to roasted chicken (165 kcal/100g). High sodium is the main concern. Removing skin and breading before eating saves 30-40% of calories.
Fried chicken vs roasted chicken?
Fried (100g with skin+breading): 260 kcal, 14g fat. Roasted breast (100g): 165 kcal, 3.6g fat. Frying adds ~95 kcal/100g primarily from oil absorbed into breading.
How many calories in KFC?
Original Recipe breast: 390 kcal. Drumstick: 130 kcal. Thigh: 310 kcal. Extra Crispy breast: 470 kcal. Chicken sandwich: 530 kcal.
Is fried chicken keto?
The chicken itself with minimal breading is relatively low carb (~8g/100g). Some keto diets include fried chicken with just egg/cheese breading instead of flour.
How to make fried chicken healthier?
Air fry instead of deep fry (saves 50-60% fat), use whole-wheat breadcrumbs, reduce salt, remove skin before eating, use yogurt marinade instead of buttermilk.
