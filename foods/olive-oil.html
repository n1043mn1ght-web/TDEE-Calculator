Olive Oil Health Benefits

PREDIMED Trial — 30% Cardiovascular Risk Reduction

The PREDIMED trial (7,447 participants, 5 years) found Mediterranean diet supplemented with 1 litre/week of EVOO reduced major cardiovascular events by 30% compared to a low-fat diet — one of the largest effect sizes ever seen in a dietary intervention trial.

Polyphenols — Anti-Inflammatory Action

EVOO contains 30-50mg/kg of polyphenols including oleuropein, hydroxytyrosol, and tyrosol. These inhibit the same inflammatory pathways as ibuprofen, reduce NF-κB activation, and lower circulating CRP and IL-6. Research suggests ~4 tablespoons EVOO daily provides anti-inflammatory effects comparable to low-dose ibuprofen.

Oleic Acid and Cardiovascular Health

At 73% monounsaturated fat (oleic acid), olive oil has the highest oleic acid content of any commonly used cooking oil. Oleic acid replaces saturated fat in cell membranes (improving insulin sensitivity), reduces LDL oxidation, raises HDL, and reduces inflammatory gene expression.

Vitamin E and K Content

Olive oil provides 14.4mg of vitamin E per 100ml (96% DV) and 60.2µg of vitamin K (50% DV). Olive oil-dressed salads significantly increase vitamin E and K intake compared to fat-free dressings, and the fat also enhances absorption of fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) and carotenoids from vegetables.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in olive oil?
Olive oil has 884 calories per 100ml. 1 tablespoon (14ml): 119 kcal. 1 teaspoon (5ml): 40 kcal. All fats have the same calorie density (9 kcal/gram).
Is olive oil healthy?
Extra virgin olive oil is widely considered a nutritious cooking oil. The PREDIMED trial found EVOO supplementation reduced cardiovascular events by 30%. Rich in monounsaturated fat and polyphenols. Still very calorie-dense — measure rather than pour freely.
How much olive oil per day?
The Mediterranean diet uses ~4-6 tablespoons daily (480-720 kcal from oil). For weight management, 1-3 tablespoons (120-360 kcal) is typical. Quality (extra virgin) matters more than quantity.
EVOO vs regular olive oil?
Extra virgin: cold-pressed, unrefined, 30-50mg/kg polyphenols, fruity flavour. Regular/light: refined, fewer polyphenols, more neutral flavour, higher smoke point. For health benefits, EVOO is superior.
Is olive oil keto?
Yes — zero carbs. One of the best keto cooking oils.
Can you cook with EVOO?
Yes — smoke point ~200°C, sufficient for most cooking. For very high heat (>220°C), refined olive oil or avocado oil is better. Polyphenols are partially degraded at high temperatures but oleic acid remains stable.
