Dark Chocolate Health Benefits

Richest Magnesium Food on Earth

Dark chocolate (85%) provides 228mg of magnesium per 100g — 54% DV — the highest of any commonly consumed food, exceeding pumpkin seeds. A 40g serving provides 91mg (22% DV). Given that magnesium deficiency affects ~50% of adults, dark chocolate is one of the most enjoyable strategies for improving magnesium status.

Iron — 67% Daily Value

Dark chocolate provides 12mg of iron per 100g — 67% DV — one of the highest iron concentrations of any food, even exceeding beef liver per equal weight. This is non-haem iron, but the quantity is so high that meaningful amounts are absorbed. Eating with vitamin C-rich foods increases absorption 2-6×.

Cardiovascular Evidence — 40+ Clinical Trials

A 2017 meta-analysis of 40 RCTs found dark cocoa significantly reduces systolic blood pressure by 3-4 mmHg and diastolic by 2-3 mmHg — comparable to some antihypertensive medications. Mechanisms include flavonoid-induced nitric oxide production, reduced platelet aggregation, and improved endothelial function.

Low Glycemic Index — 23

Despite 46g of carbohydrates per 100g, dark chocolate has a GI of approximately 23 — very low for a sweet food. Fat slows gastric emptying, fiber (10.9g/100g) slows carbohydrate absorption, and polyphenols inhibit digestive enzymes — making dark chocolate one of the better sweet options for blood sugar management.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in dark chocolate?
Dark chocolate (70-85%) has 598 calories per 100g. A 30g portion: 179 kcal. Per square (10g): 60 kcal.
Is dark chocolate a nutrient-dense food?
It has genuine nutrient-dense food qualities: richest magnesium food source (54% DV/100g), high iron (67% DV), powerful flavonoids, and clinically proven cardiovascular benefits at 30-40g/day.
How much dark chocolate per day?
30-40g of 70%+ dark chocolate provides cardiovascular benefits at ~180-240 kcal — the amount used in most positive clinical trials.
Dark chocolate vs milk chocolate?
Dark 70%: 598 kcal, 10.9g fiber, 24g sugar, 228mg magnesium. Milk: 535 kcal, 3.4g fiber, 51g sugar, 176mg magnesium. Dark is significantly more nutritious.
Is dark chocolate keto?
85%+: ~15g net carbs/100g. 70%: ~35g net carbs. Choose 85%+ for keto.
Does dark chocolate have caffeine?
Yes — 100g contains ~43mg caffeine and ~450mg theobromine. A 30g serving has ~13mg caffeine — about 1/7 of a coffee.
