Bacon Nutrition Facts

Sodium — The Primary Health Concern

Bacon contains 1,717mg of sodium per 100g (75% daily limit). Three rashers (24g) contain 412mg — 18% of the daily limit. Health impact depends entirely on portion size. Uncured bacon still contains high sodium from salt cure. People with hypertension should be particularly cautious.

Nitrates and Processed Meat Research

Bacon is cured with sodium nitrate/nitrite — the WHO classifies processed meats as Group 1 carcinogens based on increased colorectal cancer risk. Each 50g daily serving increases risk by ~18%. At occasional normal portion sizes, absolute risk increase is small but non-zero.

Per Serving vs Per 100g

The per-100g figures (541 kcal, 42g fat, 1717mg sodium) look alarming but nobody eats 100g of bacon. Three rashers (24g) provide 130 kcal, 10g fat, 412mg sodium — modest contribution to daily intake in context. Treat bacon as a flavouring agent rather than a primary protein source.

Fat Composition

Bacon's 42g fat per 100g is ~38% saturated, 46% monounsaturated (oleic acid — same as olive oil), 12% polyunsaturated. The monounsaturated component is beneficial. Saturated fat level (~16g/100g, ~4g per rasher) warrants consideration for cardiovascular-risk individuals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in bacon?
Cooked bacon: 541 calories per 100g — but 1 rasher is only 8g (43 kcal). 3 rashers: 130 kcal. The per-100g figure is high because bacon loses most of its water during cooking.
How many calories in 3 rashers of bacon?
3 rashers (24g cooked): ~130 kcal, 9g protein, 10g fat, 412mg sodium.
Is bacon keto?
Yes — essentially zero carbs (1.4g/100g). Popular keto food for high fat and protein content.
Is bacon healthy?
Occasionally in small amounts, not significantly harmful. Concerns: high sodium, processed meat (modest cancer risk at high intake), high saturated fat. As occasional flavouring, risk is low.
Is turkey bacon healthier?
Turkey bacon: ~200 kcal/100g vs 541. Less fat but still processed meat with high sodium. Better for calories and saturated fat.
How much sodium in bacon?
1,717mg/100g. One rasher (8g): ~137mg. Three rashers: ~412mg — 18% of daily 2,300mg limit.
