White Bread Nutrition Facts

High Glycemic Index — Rapid Blood Sugar Impact

White bread GI ~75, similar to glucose (100), significantly higher than whole wheat (69), pasta (43-50), or even white rice (73). Fine particle size means starch is rapidly accessible, producing fast blood sugar peaks. This rapid glycemic response stimulates large insulin releases that can promote fat storage and quickly restore hunger.

Enrichment — Partial Nutrition Restoration

White flour loses ~20 nutrients during milling. Enrichment restores thiamine, riboflavin, niacin, folate, and iron — but not fiber, vitamin E, magnesium, zinc, or phytochemicals. Enriched white bread is superior to unenriched but still significantly less nutritious than whole wheat. Folate enrichment has reduced neural tube defects in countries with mandatory enrichment programs.

Sodium Content

~491mg sodium per 100g — 21% of daily limit. Two sandwich slices contribute ~275mg sodium before fillings. Choose low-sodium varieties or whole grain alternatives for hypertension management.

Practical Role in Diet

Despite nutritional limitations, white bread serves important roles in food access, affordability, palatability, and feeding challenges. Enriched B vitamins and iron make it a meaningful nutritional contribution for populations with limited food variety or food insecurity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in white bread?
White bread has 267 calories per 100g. One slice (28g): 75 kcal. A sandwich (2 slices): 150 kcal from bread alone.
Is white bread unhealthy?
White bread is nutritionally modest — low fiber, high GI (75), and high sodium. Regular consumption of refined grains like white bread is associated with increased risk of obesity, type 2 diabetes, and cardiovascular disease.
White bread vs whole wheat?
Whole wheat: more fiber (6.9g vs 2.7g), lower GI (~69 vs 75), more vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants. Whole wheat is nutritionally superior in almost every measure.
Is white bread fattening?
High GI (75) causes rapid blood sugar spikes and crashes that increase hunger. However, total calorie intake determines weight. Switching to whole grain provides better satiety at similar calories.
Is white bread gluten-free?
No — contains gluten from wheat. Not suitable for celiac disease.
How many calories in a slice of bread?
Standard slice (28g): ~75 kcal, 2.1g protein, 14.2g carbs. Thick-cut (40g): ~107 kcal.
