Quick summary: Cooked pasta contains 131 calories per 100g. A cup (140g) has 183 calories with 7g protein. Pasta has a surprisingly low glycemic index (43-50) — lower than rice and bread — due to its dense structure slowing starch digestion. Al dente pasta has a lower GI than overcooked. Cold pasta has GI ~28.

Pasta Health Facts

Low Glycemic Index — Surprising Reality

Pasta has GI 43-50 — significantly lower than white rice (73), white bread (75), or brown rice (68). The dense protein-starch matrix slows enzyme access to starch. Al dente pasta (firm) has GI ~5-10 points lower than overcooked. Cooling drops GI to ~28 via resistant starch formation.

The Mediterranean Paradox

The Mediterranean diet — consistently associated with lowest cardiovascular disease rates and longest longevity — includes substantial pasta. Italians eat ~26kg of pasta per person per year yet have notably low obesity rates. Research confirms moderate pasta within a Mediterranean dietary pattern is not associated with overweight. Portion size and accompaniments are the key variables.

Enrichment — B Vitamins and Iron

Enriched pasta provides 21% of daily folate — significant for DNA synthesis and pregnancy. Thiamine, riboflavin, niacin, and iron are also restored. This ensures pasta is nutritionally meaningful, even as a refined carbohydrate.

Resistant Starch and Cold Pasta

A 2008 study found cold pasta had GI of 28 vs 48 when freshly cooked. Pasta salad and reheated pasta have lower glycemic impact than freshly cooked. This effect persists through reheating — resistant starch does not fully revert.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in pasta?
Cooked pasta has 131 calories per 100g. A cup (140g): 183 kcal and 7g protein.
Is pasta healthy?
Yes in moderate portions — low GI (43-50), enriched B vitamins, and 5g protein per 100g. The issue is usually large portions and high-calorie sauces, not pasta itself.
Is pasta good for weight loss?
In appropriate portions, yes. Low GI means slower energy release. Research suggests moderate pasta consumption is not associated with overweight in Mediterranean populations.
Is pasta keto?
No — 23.2g net carbs per 100g cooked.
Whole wheat vs regular pasta?
Whole wheat: 3.9g fiber, more minerals, GI ~42. Regular: 1.8g fiber, GI ~45-50. Whole wheat is nutritionally superior.
Does pasta spike blood sugar?
Less than expected — GI 43-50 is lower than rice or bread. Al dente cooking lowers GI further. Cooling pasta to eat cold drops GI to ~28.
