Edamame Health Benefits

Folate — 76% Daily Value Per Cup

Edamame provides 303µg of folate per 100g — 76% of the daily value, and a cup provides 116% of the daily requirement. This is the highest folate content of any legume by significant margin. Folate is essential for DNA synthesis, cell division, and neural tube development during the first trimester of pregnancy — often before women know they are pregnant. For women of reproductive age, edamame is one of the most important dietary folate sources.

Complete Protein at Low Calories

At 11.91g of complete protein per 100g at only 121 kcal, edamame has one of the best protein-to-calorie ratios of any plant food. A cup provides 18.5g of complete protein — all 9 essential amino acids in proportions matching human requirements. For vegans seeking high-quality protein without high calories, edamame is exceptional — outperforming lentils (9g/116 kcal) and chickpeas (8.86g/164 kcal) in both protein density and protein quality.

Lowest Carbs of Any Legume

With only 3.7g of net carbs per 100g, edamame is the most carbohydrate-efficient legume available. Most of its carbohydrates are fiber (5.2g/100g), with minimal available starch. This makes edamame the only legume realistically compatible with ketogenic and very low-carb diets. Its combination of complete protein, low net carbs, and healthy fat makes it nutritionally similar to an animal protein source.

Vitamin K for Bone Health

Edamame provides 26.7µg of vitamin K per 100g (22% DV) — the most vitamin K of any legume. Vitamin K activates osteocalcin — a protein that binds calcium into bone matrix — and matrix Gla-protein, which prevents calcium from depositing in arteries. Research shows vitamin K2 (found in fermented soy foods like natto and miso) is particularly potent for bone health. Edamame's K1 content still meaningfully contributes to bone health outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in edamame?
Edamame has 121 calories per 100g. A cup shelled (155g): 188 kcal and 18.5g protein.
Is edamame keto?
Yes — only 3.7g net carbs per 100g — the lowest net carbs of any legume. A cup has 5.7g net carbs. Popular keto snack for its protein content.
Is edamame a complete protein?
Yes — edamame (young soybeans) is one of the few plant foods with a complete protein profile (PDCAAS 1.0), providing all 9 essential amino acids.
Is edamame the same as soybeans?
Yes — edamame is soybeans harvested while still young and green, before they fully dry and harden. They have a milder, sweeter flavour and slightly different nutritional profile (more water, more folate, similar protein).
Is edamame good for pregnancy?
Excellent — a cup provides 76% of daily folate requirement — critical for preventing neural tube defects in early pregnancy. Also provides complete protein, iron, and magnesium — all important during pregnancy.
Is edamame healthy as a snack?
One of the healthiest snacks available — complete protein, very low net carbs, high fiber, excellent folate and vitamin K. 155g cup has 188 kcal with 18.5g protein — comparable to a chicken breast serving.
