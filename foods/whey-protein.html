Whey Protein Benefits

Highest Protein Quality — DIAAS 1.09

Whey protein has a DIAAS of approximately 1.09 — the highest of any protein source tested. This reflects both amino acid profile completeness and digestibility. Whey's superiority is particularly due to leucine content (~11% of protein) — leucine is the primary amino acid triggering muscle protein synthesis through mTOR activation.

Leucine — The Muscle Building Trigger

Whey's ~2.5g leucine per 30g serving reliably exceeds the threshold (~1.7-3g per meal) that maximally stimulates muscle protein synthesis. Plant proteins require larger servings to achieve equivalent leucine doses.

Post-Exercise Timing Evidence

Multiple meta-analyses confirm consuming 20-40g of whey protein within 0-2 hours post-exercise maximally stimulates muscle protein synthesis. The rapid absorption (peak blood amino acids at ~60-90 minutes) makes it particularly suited for immediate post-exercise recovery.

Beyond Muscles — Weight Management

Multiple RCTs show whey protein supplementation with calorie restriction significantly reduces body fat while preserving muscle mass — superior to carbohydrate supplementation at the same calories. Protein's satiety effect reduces subsequent calorie intake and improves dietary adherence during weight loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in whey protein?
Whey protein powder has 370 calories per 100g. Per 30g scoop: 111 kcal with 24g protein.
Is whey protein worth taking?
For people who struggle to meet protein targets through food, yes — particularly athletes, older adults, and those in calorie deficit. It's the highest-quality protein source per calorie. For people already meeting protein needs through food, it's not necessary.
How much per day?
1.6-2.2g protein per kg body weight for athletes. 1-2 scoops (24-48g protein) per day is typical supplementation in addition to food.
Whey concentrate vs isolate?
Concentrate (~80% protein): 370 kcal, more fat, some lactose. Isolate (~90% protein): 360 kcal, less fat, less lactose, faster absorption. For lactose-intolerant, isolate is better tolerated.
Does whey cause kidney damage?
No — not in people with healthy kidneys. Multiple reviews confirm high protein intake from whey does not damage kidney function in healthy adults.
Whey vs plant protein?
Whey DIAAS ~1.09 (highest), complete, rapidly absorbed. Pea+rice blend DIAAS ~0.82-0.90, complete when blended. Whey has edge for muscle synthesis; plant is comparable in adequate amounts.
