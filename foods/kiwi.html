Quick summary: Kiwi contains 61 calories per 100g and 46 calories per medium fruit. It has the highest vitamin C content of any common fruit (92.7mg/100g — 103% of daily value) and significant vitamin K (34% DV). Clinical trials show eating two kiwis before bed improves sleep onset by 35% and duration by 13%. With a low GI of ~52 and 3g fiber per 100g, kiwi is suitable for most dietary patterns.

Kiwi Health Benefits & Nutrition

Highest Vitamin C of Any Common Fruit per 100g

Kiwi contains 92.7mg of vitamin C per 100g — more than oranges (53mg), strawberries (58.8mg), or any other common fruit. A single medium kiwi (76g) provides 70mg of vitamin C, essentially meeting the adult daily requirement in one fruit. Two kiwis provide more than double the daily value. Kiwi's vitamin C is particularly bioavailable due to the presence of other antioxidants that protect ascorbic acid from oxidation.

Kiwi and Sleep — Surprising Clinical Evidence

A 2011 randomised study from Taiwan found that adults who ate two kiwis one hour before bed for 4 weeks fell asleep 35% faster, slept 13% longer, and had 5% better sleep efficiency compared to baseline. The proposed mechanism involves kiwi's high serotonin content (which is a precursor to melatonin) and antioxidants reducing oxidative stress that disrupts sleep architecture.

Cardiovascular Benefits

A Norwegian trial found that eating 2–3 kiwis daily for 28 days reduced platelet aggregation (blood clotting tendency) by 18% and triglycerides by 15%, comparable to the effects of a daily aspirin but without side effects. These effects are attributed to kiwi's vitamin C, vitamin K, potassium, and unique polyphenols including actinidin.

Skin Health and Collagen Synthesis

Vitamin C is essential for collagen synthesis — the structural protein that maintains skin elasticity, joint cartilage, and blood vessel walls. With 103% of the daily vitamin C requirement in 100g, kiwi is one of the most efficient fruits for supporting collagen production. Studies on vitamin C intake consistently show that adequate vitamin C reduces skin wrinkling, improves wound healing, and protects against UV-induced photoageing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in a kiwi?
Kiwi contains 61 calories per 100g. A medium kiwi (76g) has 46 calories. A cup of sliced kiwi (180g) has 110 calories. Use the slider above to calculate for any weight.
Can you eat kiwi skin?
Yes — kiwi skin is edible and nutritious. It contains additional fiber, vitamin C, and antioxidants. Eating the whole kiwi with skin increases fiber intake by approximately 50%. Always wash kiwis thoroughly before eating with skin.
Is kiwi high in vitamin C?
Yes — kiwi has the highest vitamin C content of any common fruit per 100g (92.7mg). A single medium kiwi provides about 77% of the adult daily vitamin C requirement. Kiwi contains more vitamin C than oranges (53mg) or strawberries (58.8mg) per 100g.
Is kiwi good for sleep?
Clinical evidence suggests yes. A 2011 study found that eating two kiwis one hour before bed for 4 weeks improved sleep onset (35% faster), duration (13% longer), and efficiency (5% better). The proposed mechanism involves kiwi's serotonin content and antioxidants.
Is kiwi keto?
Not for strict keto. Kiwi has 11.7g of net carbs per 100g. A small half-kiwi (~38g) provides about 4.5g net carbs and might fit into a flexible low-carb diet.
Are golden kiwis different from green kiwis?
Yes. Golden kiwis are sweeter, less tart, and have smoother skin. They contain slightly more vitamin C (~161mg/100g vs 92.7mg). Both varieties have comparable calories (~61 kcal/100g).
