Turkey Breast Benefits

Highest Protein-to-Calorie Ratio of Any Meat

Turkey breast provides 30.1g protein at only 135 kcal — 0.89g protein per calorie (89% of calories from protein). Chicken breast achieves 0.75g/kcal. A 170g serving provides 51g protein — the full adult daily requirement — at only 230 calories. This exceptional efficiency makes turkey breast the #1 choice for cutting phases in bodybuilding and for general weight loss.

Tryptophan — Sleep and Mood

Turkey is the richest common dietary source of tryptophan — the precursor to serotonin (mood regulation) and melatonin (sleep regulation). Evidence suggests tryptophan supplementation improves sleep quality and reduces depression symptoms. Regular turkey consumption provides meaningful dietary tryptophan supporting both mood and sleep quality.

Selenium — Best Meat Source

Turkey breast provides 31.5µg selenium/100g (57% DV) — more than chicken breast (27.6µg) or beef (28µg). Selenium is critical for antioxidant enzyme function, thyroid hormone metabolism, and male fertility. Turkey is one of the most selenium-efficient meats.

Ideal for Cutting and Body Recomposition

With only 135 kcal and 30g protein per 100g, turkey breast creates maximum anabolic stimulus while minimising calorie intake. It is the preferred protein of competitive bodybuilders during cutting phases. Research on body recomposition identifies very high protein as the key variable — turkey delivers this most efficiently.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in turkey breast?
Cooked skinless turkey breast: 135 calories per 100g — lowest of any common meat. A 3oz serving (85g): 115 kcal and 25.6g protein. A 6oz serving: 230 kcal and 51g protein.
Does turkey make you sleepy?
Turkey is the richest dietary source of tryptophan. Post-Thanksgiving drowsiness is mainly from overeating, alcohol, and carbs — but turkey does provide tryptophan that supports sleep and mood.
Is turkey breast healthier than chicken breast?
Turkey is slightly leaner (1.0g vs 3.6g fat) and lower in calories (135 vs 165 kcal) with similar protein (30g vs 31g). Turkey has more selenium and potassium.
How much protein in turkey breast?
30.1g per 100g. A 6oz serving provides 51g — essentially the full adult daily protein requirement.
Is deli turkey breast healthy?
Nutritionally similar but very high sodium (500-800mg/100g vs 63mg fresh). Choose low-sodium varieties. Fresh-cooked turkey breast is significantly healthier.
Can you eat turkey breast every day?
Yes — very safe to eat daily. Low in purines, very lean, and nutritionally rich. Complement with other proteins for dietary variety.
