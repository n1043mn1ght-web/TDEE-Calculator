Couscous Nutrition Facts

5-Minute Preparation — Ultimate Convenience

Instant couscous absorbs boiling water in 5 minutes — faster than rice (20 min), pasta (8-12 min), or quinoa (15 min). For time-pressed meal preparation, couscous is the most practical carbohydrate base for quick balanced meals with pre-cooked protein and vegetables.

Selenium Content

22.3µg per 100g (41% DV) — one of the better grain sources of this important mineral essential for antioxidant enzymes, thyroid hormone metabolism, and DNA synthesis. A cup covers 41% of the daily selenium requirement.

Near Zero Fat

0.16g fat per 100g — essentially fat-free. All calories come from carbohydrates and protein. Combine with healthy fats (olive oil, avocado) and protein for balanced nutrition and satiety.

North African Culinary Tradition

National dish of Morocco, Algeria, and Tunisia. Traditional preparation over meat and vegetable stew (tagine) significantly enhances the overall meal's nutrition. Mediterranean and North African dietary patterns including couscous are associated with longevity and low chronic disease rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in couscous?
Cooked couscous has 112 calories per 100g. A cup (157g): 176 calories.
Is couscous healthy?
Moderate nutrition — good selenium, low fat, very quick to prepare. Less nutritious than quinoa (no complete protein, less fiber, less magnesium) but extremely convenient.
Is couscous gluten-free?
No — made from semolina (durum wheat). Not suitable for celiac disease. Quinoa is a gluten-free alternative.
Is couscous good for weight loss?
Moderate — 112 kcal/100g with very low fat. However, low fiber (1.4g/100g) means less filling than higher-fiber grains. Pair with protein and vegetables.
Couscous vs quinoa?
Quinoa: more protein (4.4g vs 3.8g), more fiber (2.8g vs 1.4g), more magnesium, complete protein, gluten-free. Couscous: faster (5 vs 15 min), more selenium. For nutrition, quinoa is superior.
What is couscous made from?
Couscous is made from semolina — coarsely ground durum wheat endosperm. It is tiny pasta pellets, not a grain itself. Instant couscous rehydrates in boiling water in 5 minutes.
