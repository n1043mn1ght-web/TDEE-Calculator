Shrimp Health Benefits

Best Protein-to-Calorie Ratio in Seafood

Shrimp provides 24g of protein at only 99 kcal per 100g — with a protein-to-calorie ratio of 0.97, only turkey breast and egg whites achieve comparable efficiency. The nearly zero fat content (0.28g/100g) means virtually all calories come from protein. Six large shrimp provide 20g of complete protein at only 83 calories.

Cholesterol Misconception

Shrimp is high in dietary cholesterol (189mg/100g) but research consistently shows this does not raise blood cholesterol for most people. The landmark Framingham Heart Study and subsequent meta-analyses found no association between egg and shellfish consumption and cardiovascular disease. Shrimp is low in saturated fat — the actual driver of LDL cholesterol elevation.

Astaxanthin — Unique Marine Antioxidant

Shrimp contains astaxanthin — the same carotenoid that gives salmon its pink colour. Astaxanthin is 6,000× more potent as an antioxidant than vitamin C and uniquely accumulates in mitochondria. It reduces exercise-induced muscle damage, protects skin from UV damage, reduces markers of cardiovascular risk, and crosses the blood-brain barrier to provide neuroprotective effects.

Iodine for Thyroid Health

Shrimp is one of the best dietary sources of iodine — a mineral essential for thyroid hormone synthesis that is often deficient in inland populations. Iodine deficiency is the leading cause of preventable intellectual disability worldwide. Regular shrimp consumption reliably maintains iodine status, particularly important for pregnant women whose iodine requirements increase by 50%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in shrimp?
Shrimp has 99 calories per 100g cooked. Six large shrimp (84g): 83 kcal and 20g protein. Shrimp is one of the lowest calorie protein sources.
Is shrimp high in cholesterol?
Yes — shrimp has 189mg of dietary cholesterol per 100g. However, Research suggests dietary cholesterol has minimal impact on blood cholesterol for most people. The main drivers of blood cholesterol are saturated fat and trans fat, not dietary cholesterol.
Is shrimp good for weight loss?
Excellent — 99 kcal per 100g with 24g protein and nearly zero fat. Very high protein-to-calorie ratio creates strong satiety at minimal caloric cost.
Is shrimp keto?
Yes — virtually zero carbs (0.24g/100g) with high protein and minimal fat. Shrimp is excellent for keto.
Is shrimp safe during pregnancy?
Yes — shrimp is classified as 'best choice' seafood by the FDA/EPA due to very low mercury content. 2-3 servings/week are recommended as safe during pregnancy.
How many shrimp is a serving?
A standard 3oz (85g) serving is approximately 4-6 large shrimp or 8-10 medium shrimp, providing ~25g protein at ~85 calories.
