Chicken Wings Nutrition

Calories: Plain vs Sauced vs Breaded

Plain roasted: 290 kcal/100g. Buffalo (hot sauce + butter): ~340. BBQ: ~360. Breaded fried: ~420. Honey glazed: ~370. A 6-8 wing restaurant order ranges from 600 kcal (plain) to 1,200+ (breaded with dipping sauce). Preparation is the single biggest calorie variable.

Skin Calories — The Key Variable

Most fat in wings comes from the skin. Removing skin after cooking cuts calories by ~40%, from 290 to ~175 kcal/100g. Baking renders more fat from the skin than frying, reducing net calories absorbed.

Protein Content

Despite lower protein-to-calorie ratio than breast (due to fat), wings still provide 27g protein/100g. A 6-wing serving provides ~55g protein. Wings also contain more collagen from skin and cartilage than breast.

Bone-In Benefits

Cooking bone-in releases collagen and minerals into the meat and cooking juices. Cartilage near the bone provides chondroitin and glucosamine — compounds associated with joint health.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in chicken wings?
Plain cooked wings: 290 calories per 100g. One wing (34g): ~99 kcal. 6 wings plain: ~590 kcal. Breaded with sauce: up to 1,200+ for 6 wings.
How many calories in buffalo wings?
Buffalo wings: ~320-350 kcal/100g, ~110-120 kcal per wing. A 6-wing order: ~650-700 kcal before dipping sauce.
Are chicken wings high in protein?
Yes — 27g protein per 100g. A 6-wing serving provides ~55g protein when eating all meat.
Are chicken wings keto?
Plain (unbreaded, unsauced) wings are fully keto — zero carbs. Buffalo sauce adds minimal carbs. BBQ sauce adds 5-8g carbs per wing.
How many wings is a serving?
Standard: 3oz (85g) ≈ 2-3 whole wings. Restaurant orders: 6-10 wings.
Baked vs fried wings?
Baked at 200°C renders fat from skin, saving 50-100 kcal per 100g vs fried. Pat dry before baking for crispiness.
