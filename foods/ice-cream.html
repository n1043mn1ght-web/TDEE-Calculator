Ice Cream Nutrition Facts

Calcium — Dairy Benefit

Ice cream provides 128mg of calcium per 100g (12% DV). A two-scoop serving provides ~170mg (17% DV). While better calcium sources exist (Greek yogurt, milk, cheese), ice cream's calcium content is a genuine positive in what is primarily an indulgence food.

Sugar Content — Primary Concern

At 21g sugar per 100g, ice cream delivers rapid glucose and fructose into the bloodstream. A two-scoop serving contains ~27g — more than the WHO's recommended daily maximum. This rapid sugar load promotes insulin spikes and, combined with saturated fat, creates an unfavourable metabolic combination.

Portion Size — The Key Variable

The calorie difference between a reasonable portion and a typical restaurant serving is enormous. One scoop (65g): 135 kcal. Two scoops in a waffle cone with hot fudge and whipped cream: 600-900 kcal. Measuring at home and eating mindfully is the most effective strategy for including ice cream in a balanced diet.

Healthier Alternatives

Protein ice creams (Halo Top: 80-110 kcal/100g, 10-15g protein), frozen yogurt (120 kcal), sorbet (130 kcal, no fat), and homemade banana 'nice cream' (90 kcal, no added sugar) allow ice cream-like satisfaction with significantly fewer calories.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in ice cream?
Vanilla ice cream has 207 calories per 100g. Per scoop (65g): 135 kcal. Ben & Jerry's: ~260-320 kcal/100g. Gelato: ~160 kcal. Sorbet: ~130 kcal.
Is ice cream healthy?
Ice cream provides calcium and protein but is high in saturated fat and sugar. As an occasional treat it's fine. Daily consumption is associated with increased cardiovascular risk and weight gain.
What is the lowest calorie ice cream?
Sorbet: ~130 kcal/100g. Frozen yogurt: ~120 kcal. Halo Top style protein ice cream: ~80-110 kcal/100g. Gelato: ~160 kcal. Regular: 207+ kcal.
Ice cream vs gelato?
Gelato: ~160 kcal/100g, 6g fat, denser, served warmer. Ice cream: 207 kcal, 11g fat, more air churned in. Gelato is slightly lighter.
Is ice cream keto?
Regular: No — 23g carbs/100g. Keto ice cream (erythritol sweetened): ~5g net carbs/100g.
How many calories in a Magnum?
Magnum Classic (110ml): ~250 kcal. Magnum Double: ~310 kcal. Magnum Mini (55ml): ~130 kcal.
