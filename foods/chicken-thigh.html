Chicken Thigh Benefits

More Iron and Zinc Than Breast

Chicken thigh is dark meat — higher myoglobin content means more iron (1.34mg vs 0.74mg/100g) and zinc (2.8mg vs 1.0mg). Iron and zinc are the two nutrients most commonly deficient in active people. For those monitoring these minerals, thigh is significantly superior to breast.

Fat Quality — Mostly Unsaturated

Despite 10.9g of fat per 100g, thigh fat is ~56% monounsaturated (oleic acid — same as olive oil) and 20% polyunsaturated, with only 24% saturated. The monounsaturated component is associated with cardiovascular benefits and improves absorption of fat-soluble vitamins.

Flavour and Dietary Adherence

Research shows food palatability is one of the strongest predictors of long-term diet success. Thigh's higher fat gives significantly more flavour and juiciness than breast — harder to overcook and remains tender across a wider range of cooking methods, improving dietary adherence.

Collagen for Joint Health

Chicken thighs, particularly bone-in when cooked in soups or braises, release collagen — glycine-rich protein supporting joint cartilage and gut lining integrity. Clinical studies show dietary collagen peptides benefit joint pain in athletes and osteoarthritis patients.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories in chicken thigh?
Cooked skinless chicken thigh has 209 calories per 100g. A medium bone-in thigh (116g) has 242 calories. Skin-on adds 40-60 kcal per serving.
Is chicken thigh healthier than breast?
Both are nutritious. Breast has more protein (31g vs 26g) and fewer calories. Thigh has more iron, zinc, and flavour. For minerals and eating satisfaction, thigh wins.
How much protein in chicken thigh?
26g of protein per 100g cooked. A medium thigh (116g boneless) provides ~30g of protein.
Is chicken thigh keto?
Yes — zero carbohydrates. The higher fat content is actually advantageous for keto.
Skin-on vs skinless thigh calories?
Skinless: ~209 kcal/100g. Skin-on: ~270-290 kcal/100g. Removing skin reduces calories by ~25%.
Does chicken thigh have more fat than breast?
Yes — 10.9g vs 3.6g. But the fat is predominantly unsaturated (similar to olive oil) and provides flavour and satiety.
